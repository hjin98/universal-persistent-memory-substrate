## 2. Proposal

| ID | Proposal | Answers | Why it needs the owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **D-97** | **A resolution settles an objection only for the target it assessed, as far as scientific standing goes.** D-94 keeps one resolution record, which closes the objection's workflow everywhere: nobody must resolve it again, and decisions show it as "resolved by assessment at ⟨target⟩". But for route status, claim standing and the derived predicates, an objection is **open for** every record it applies through other than the one its resolution assessed. So when an objection to `K1` is carried to `K2` and resolved by a judgment about `K2`, `K1`'s standing still shows the objection, labeled "resolved by assessment at `K2`, not assessed for `K1`", and `has-sound-support(K1)` is false. A withdrawal by the objection's author settles it for every target | SC69-02 | D-94 said one resolution "settles the objection everywhere" and also that the label means "it never reads as settling the earlier target". The two clauses conflict for the standing of `K1`; this proposal keeps the second. The alternative is D-94's first clause literally: `K1`, a refuted claim, can then show sound support, with only the label to warn the reader |
