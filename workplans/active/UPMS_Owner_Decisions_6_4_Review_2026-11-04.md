## 2. Decisions

| ID | Decision | Answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **D-79** | **Conflict-based appointment refusal is a disclosed predicate.** The core may refuse an appointment because a conflict exists that it does not disclose. Before writing anonymously, a critic is told that such a refusal can let a person with a declared relationship to them infer that they are a critic in the review. The guarantee that nothing released depends on sealed attribution is narrowed to exactly this one disclosed fact: every other count, label, predicate and check result stays independent of sealed attribution. No euphemistic reason, random delay or wrapper is added. The alternative put to the owner (appoint the chair before anonymous submissions open, and tell a conflicting critic privately that they may write only signed) was not adopted | SC64-02; TE-R13, TE-R14, R-09, F62-11 |
| **D-80** | **Objection coverage is stated uniformly; audiences are rechecked at the acts that move objections.** Every standing report, decision projection and question page states, by a rule that does not depend on whether any hidden objection exists, when the reader's access could hide an objection: when the reader reads the target only through an own-record entitlement, or does not read every scope that may home objections to it. A rebind of an objection and a share or copy of a target recheck that the objection's home is readable by every reader of the new target. Permission changes are never refused because of objections | SC64-03; SC-42, TE-R08 |
| **D-81** | **Support depends on direction.** Only a route that bears positively on the claim counts towards support of it. A usable `refutes`, `disfavored`, `inadequate`, `prediction-failed`, `consistent-with` or `bears-on` relation is reported as a usable route of its own kind, never as support. No score is added | R64-01; TE-R11 |
| **D-82** | **Disagreement about compatibility leaves it unestablished.** While effective comparability judgments disagree on whether a condition covers another, compatibility is not established; the route shows "compatibility disputed" with both judgments, and neither is chosen | R64-02; F62-03 |
| **D-83** | **Objections to a withdrawn record apply to any decision that cites it.** A new decision may still cite a withdrawn record; it is labeled "cites a withdrawn record", and the record's objections join its applicable set. Nothing is refused | R64-03 |
| **D-84** | **D-65 stands, with its availability cost.** The recovery log's objection grounds and resolution bases are under the ordinary erasure rules on every surface, including the off-site copy. The core states the records each log entry needs alongside it on restore and how a missing one is shown, and a protected commit is never acknowledged while the off-site copy is unreachable | R64-04; F62-07, F62-17 |
| **D-85** | **Simplify by owner, not by exception.** The change-history appendices of the D1 text move to a companion file; the core's drafting history in §1 and §18 is cut to bindings; the objection applicability rule is defined once in D1 and cited everywhere else; the scattered absolute sealing statements are replaced by one stated guarantee | Review §7 |
