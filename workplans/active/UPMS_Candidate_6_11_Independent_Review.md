### 1.1 Defeat of SC610-01 (Dispute settlement deadlock)

The counterexample trajectory of SC610-01 demonstrated that under Candidate 1.10 Definition 2.5, a dispute $O'$ resolved by an assessment $R_2$ assessing resolution $R$ remained permanently open against the underlying claim $K$, because $R_2$ did not assess $K$.

In D1 Candidate 1.11 Definition 2.5:
> "In a view over a footprint $F$ (F-6), $O$ **applies through** each record of $\text{bind}(O) \cap F$; a dispute, which F-6 adds through the resolution it disputes, **applies through that resolution**. $O$ is **settled for** a record $x$ it applies through when $r(O) = \text{withdrawn}$, or when $r(O) = \text{resolved-by-assessment}$, the resolution assessed $x$, and that resolution is not disputed (F-4); otherwise $O$ is **open for** $x$. $O$ is **open in** the view when it is open for some record it applies through there."

**Retested trajectory:**
1. Claim $K$ has objection $O$.
2. Resolution decision $R$ resolves $O$, assessing target $K$.
3. Critic $C$ files dispute $O'$ targeting $R$.
   - Under Definition 2.5, in a view over footprint $F$ presenting $K$, dispute $O'$ applies through resolution $R$.
   - While $O'$ is unresolved ($r(O')$ absent), $O'$ is open for $R$, so $O'$ is open in the view.
   - Under F-4, resolution $R$ is disputed because $O'$ targets $R$ and is open for $R$.
   - Because $R$ is disputed, $O$ is not settled for $K$. $O$ applies through $K$, so $O$ is also open in the view.
   - Any route supporting $K$ has status `challenged` (§6.1); `has-sound-support(K)` is false; `has-open-challenge(K)` is true (F-28).
4. An independent adjudication commits resolution decision $R_2$, resolving dispute $O'$ by assessing $R$. $R_2$ is undisputed.
5. Re-evaluate dispute $O'$:
   - $O'$ applies through $R$. $r(O') = \text{resolved-by-assessment}$, resolution $R_2$ assessed $R$, and $R_2$ is not disputed.
   - Therefore, $O'$ is **settled for $R$**.
   - $O'$ is not open for $R$, and $O'$ applies through no other record in the view.
   - Therefore, $O'$ is **not open in the view**.
6. Re-evaluate resolution $R$:
   - Under F-4, a resolution is disputed while some objection that targets it is open for it.
   - Because $O'$ is settled for $R$, $O'$ is no longer open for $R$.
   - Therefore, $R$ is **no longer disputed**.
7. Re-evaluate original objection $O$:
   - $O$ applies through $K$. $r(O) = \text{resolved-by-assessment}$, $R$ assessed $K$, and $R$ is no longer disputed.
   - Therefore, $O$ is **settled for $K$**.
   - $O$ is not open for $K$, and $O$ applies through no other record in the view.
   - Therefore, $O$ is **not open in the view**.
8. Re-evaluate claim standing predicates (D1 §6.1, F-28):
   - Neither $O$ nor $O'$ is open in the view.
   - Route supporting $K$ is **no longer challenged**; it evaluates to `assessed-sound` (if premises are established).
   - `has-open-challenge(K)` is **false**.
   - `has-sound-support(K)` is **true**.
   - In Core §15.1, Acceptance Scenario T134 and D1 stress case 31 execute cleanly without contradiction.

SC610-01 is **completely defeated**.

## 3. Other findings by earliest owning domain

### R611-01 — Informational wording in Acceptance Scenario T83 uses shorthand "unresolved objection" rather than citing §12.2's liveness predicate

**Earliest owner:** D3 Core §15.1 (Acceptance Scenario T83); downstream of Core §12.2.  
**Severity:** Low / Informational consistency (non-blocking).

Core §12.2 defines the liveness predicate that prevents records from being archived:
> "A record is **live** if any of these holds:
> - it is cited, by exact revision or as a route endpoint, by an active claim, inference step, evidence relation, decision basis or coordination record;
> - it is a position or outcome of an open question;
> - **it is an objection open for a record it applies through (D1 Definition 2.5), or the target of one (a withdrawn target included, D1 F-3b)**;
> - it is an effective judgment (D1 Definition 6.0) of a claim or route..."

In Core §15.1, Acceptance Scenario T83 summarizes archive protections:
> "| T83 | A year of simulated operation with no requests: nothing is deleted. A refuted claim is recommended for archive with its reason; **a record cited by a live claim, an unresolved objection, an open question's position, a pinned record and an open task's checkpoint are never recommended and the core refuses to archive them**; recency of use alone recommends nothing..."

While an unresolved objection ($r(O)$ absent) is open for every record in $\text{bind}(O)$, an objection that has been resolved at $K_2$ remains open for $K_1$ under D1 Definition 2.5 and D-97. Under §12.2, such an objection is live (and $K_1$ is live) and the core refuses to archive it. T83's phrase "an unresolved objection" is an informal shorthand that omits resolved carried objections that remain open for predecessor claims.

**Bounded repair (for future maintenance):**  
In Core §15.1 T83, align the wording with §12.2:
> "...a record cited by a live claim, an objection open for a record it applies through, an open question's position..."
