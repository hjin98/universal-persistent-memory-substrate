**Reading of the ratified text.** D2 1.3 §10.3 proposes its values "for the owner to set before measurement", and core 6.21 §16 requires "evaluation frozen before measurement". Under D-122 both mean before the **qualifying** measurement on held-out data: the procedure is frozen at Gate 0, the values after calibration on development data. Calibration uses no held-out data, so this keeps the separation those texts protect.

## 3. Corrections to what the owner was shown

Two statements in the recommendation were too strong; the D2 1.4 draft follows the corrected forms.

- **Independence.** "About 300 or more planted-fact questions" from "at least 100 held-out episodes" does not show a 1% limit if failures cluster within episodes: with 100 episodes, three bad episodes out of a hundred escape detection about 5% of the time. A 1% limit needs about 300 independent episodes (exactly 299 with no failure, 473 with one), counting a fixed number of questions of a class per episode and treating the episode as failed if any of them fails. Reporting both per-question and per-episode counts is kept, but acceptance uses the episode count.
- **Real use.** The recommendation said real use "either confirms or reopens" the long-run target. Errors found through users' corrections are a lower bound on real errors, since unnoticed errors are not counted, so real use can reopen a target but cannot confirm it. Confirmation from real use needs an audited random sample of real answers, which needs the permission core §15.2 names.
