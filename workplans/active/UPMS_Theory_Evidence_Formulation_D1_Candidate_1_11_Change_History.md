#### Definition 2.5, settled-for paragraph

One resolution closes `O`'s workflow everywhere it applies: nobody must resolve it again (owner decision D-94). For scientific standing, `O` is **settled for** a record `x` it applies through when `r(O) = withdrawn`, or when `r(O) = resolved-by-assessment` and the resolution assessed `x`, and its resolution is not disputed (F-4); otherwise `O` is **open for** `x`. A projection that shows `O` through a record other than the one the resolution assessed says so, for example "resolved by assessment at `K2`, not assessed for `K1`", so it never reads as settling the earlier target (D-94; D-97, **pending**; Codex reviews SC67-03, SC68-01; Gemini review SC69-02).
