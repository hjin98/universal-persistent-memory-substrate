## 1. Serious Challenges

### SC613-01 — An empty-premise inference manufactures an event root

**Earliest owner:** D1 §6.1, “Roots and eligible support,” constrained by §5.8 F-25. Downstream: core §§5.3 and 9.9; F-28; TE-07; T135. D-70 and D-71 must remain coherent with the repair.

**Blocking formulation defect; high confidence in the literal result.**

D1 §6.1 allows rooting when a supporting judgment:

> cites an inference step `R'` concluding `Y` each of whose premises is rooted for `R'` avoiding `X`.

It calls this:

> the least such relation over the records committed at `p`

But F-25 requires:

> Support that is circular, or that has no applicable root in events, is not a warrant, whoever produced it.

The inference branch has no requirement that it actually reach a terminal basis. Its universal condition succeeds when the cited step has no premises.

**Counterexample trajectory.** The graph and record construction below are paraphrases.

1. Record distinct claims P and K. For example, P states that a reading equals 42; K states that the reading is positive.
2. Assert a step Z with no premises, concluding P. Z cites no event. Nothing in Definition 2.7 or the core’s `record_inference` contract forbids such a step.
3. Record an effective `supported` claim judgment Jₚ of P whose sole supporting reference is Z. All scope, ruleset, revision and validity guards hold.
4. Assert the valid step S: P → K, and give S an effective positive inference assessment.
5. No adverse judgment, objection, pending obligation or carried condition affects S.
6. Evaluate whether P is rooted for S avoiding `{K}`.

P is outside the avoided set. Jₚ cites Z, which concludes P. The remaining condition is:

```text
every premise of Z is rooted for Z avoiding {K}
```

Z has no premises, so this condition is true. P enters the least rooting relation without any event seed. Consequently P is established for S, S is `assessed-sound`, and F-28 makes `has-sound-support(K)` true.

The events recording that these assertions occurred are not cited as supporting events. Their existence does not supply the missing terminal basis.

Z need not even have a positive inference assessment: the rooting paragraph expressly excludes path validity from its calculation. The unsupported assertion obtains the required structure by being recorded as an empty-premise inference.

**Sibling trajectory: the empty-premise alternative clears an actual converse cycle.**

Add U: K → P and another eligible supporting judgment of P citing U. Without Z, S is circular. With Z, P is rooted avoiding K, so the converse cycle ceases to disqualify S. No independent event root has been added.

This reproduces the consequence of SC612-01 through a different admitted graph shape. The original P–Q examples are repaired; the general root requirement is not.

**Interaction with assumptions and discharge.** D1 §6.1 also removes discharged hypotheses and carried conditions from the premises requiring establishment. D-70 and D-71 therefore make an empty *effective* premise set a legitimate boundary even if raw inference records were later required to contain an entry. Simply prohibiting syntactically empty premise lists would not close the semantic boundary.

Those owner decisions permit conditional reasoning without judgments establishing assumptions. They do not explicitly authorize treating an empty conjunction as an event root.

**Consequence.** The recursive clause introduces a second rooting base case: an inference with no remaining premises. That contradicts the claimed event-root characterization and can disable the cycle safeguard. Calling the relation “least” does not remove this seed.

This is a documentary classification failure, not a claim that every premise-free mathematical inference is invalid.

**Bounded repair.**

- Require a successful event-rooting witness to reach an applicable cited terminal basis. An empty-premise universal must not create that witness.
- State how premise-free, assumption-only and discharged-assumption arguments interact with F-25. Preserve their conditional validity without silently calling them event-rooted.
- Audit top-level premise-free routes as well as premise-free steps cited inside another premise’s support.
- Preserve eligibility at every depth and the distinction between rootlessness and circularity.
- Extend T135 with the empty-premise citation, the converse-cycle sibling, and an effective-empty-premise case arising from D-70/D-71.

This needs a coherent D1 definition, not another stored status or exception registry. If assumption-only arguments are intended as an exception to F-25’s event-root requirement, the owner must decide that exception explicitly.

### 4.5 Rooting does not certify validity of the support chain

The request specifically asks whether ignoring intermediate validity can allow sound status that a reader might interpret too strongly.

D1 §6.1 explicitly says:

> Rooting checks roots, eligibility and dependence on `κ`, not whether every step on the path is valid

Its Well-foundedness paragraph also says:

> Route status depends on premises only through their own **effective judgments**

**Boundary trajectory.** Assume eligible effective supporting judgments and no pending obligations:

```text
Q has a supporting judgment citing event e.
U concludes P from Q.
P has a supporting judgment citing U.
S validly concludes K from P.
U has an effective negative inference assessment.
S has an effective positive inference assessment.
```

The rooting calculation can root P through Q and e despite U’s negative assessment. P’s positive claim judgment is a separate assessment; Definition 6.0 does not automatically replace it with a negative claim assessment merely because U was judged invalid. Under the stated local rule, S can therefore be `assessed-sound` while U is broken. The report must show the conflicting upstream state at the computed depth.

**Consequence.** `has-sound-support(K)` is not a certificate that every step in an event-rooted support chain has been assessed valid. Rooting supplies a structural property; the immediate premise’s attributed assessment supplies the local epistemic input.

I do **not** count this trajectory alone as a contradiction of the explicitly local definition. Automatically importing every upstream projected route status would strengthen that definition.
